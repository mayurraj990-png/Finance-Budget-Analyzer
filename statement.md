# Problem Statement & Project Definition

## Project Title
**Personal Finance & Budget Analyzer**

## Problem Statement
Many individuals struggle to track their monthly spending and maintain a clear understanding of their financial health. Without structured tracking, it is difficult to distinguish essential expenses from discretionary spending, leading to overspending, low savings rates, and missed financial goals. Existing financial software can often be overly complex or bloated for users who simply need a quick, clear analysis of their monthly budget.

## Proposed Solution
The **Personal Finance & Budget Analyzer** is a lightweight Python command-line interface (CLI) application designed to provide immediate, actionable insights into monthly finances. By categorizing expenses into *Needs vs. Wants*, the tool automatically calculates key financial metrics, evaluates budget health against recommended benchmarks, and alerts users to high spending in specific discretionary areas.

## Objectives
* **Categorization:** Classify user expenses cleanly into essential "Needs" and optional "Wants".
* **Metric Calculation:** Compute overall total spent, remaining balance, spending percentage, and savings percentage.
* **Health Assessment:** Benchmark current user savings against standard financial targets (such as a 20% savings goal) and assign a budget health status.
* **Smart Insights:** Provide automated warnings when spending on non-essential categories (e.g., dining out, subscriptions) exceeds reasonable thresholds.

## Scope & Target Audience
* **Target Audience:** Students, young professionals, and individuals looking for a simple, privacy-focused command-line tool to analyze personal budgets.
* **Scope:** 
  * Interactive CLI input for monthly income and itemized expenses.
  * Automated summary reports generated right in the terminal.
  * Extensible structure for future features (such as data export to CSV/JSON or interactive visualization).

## Tech Stack
* **Language:** Python 3.x
* **Interface:** Command-Line Interface (CLI)q
